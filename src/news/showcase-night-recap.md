The last of our four [Showcase Nights](/programs/showcase-nights-2025/) filled the community hall in Chitungwiza on Saturday.

Twenty-two young performers and exhibitors took the stage and the walls. Sound Lab members played a full set. Visual Arts Lab graduates hung thirty works, four of which sold on the night. Creative Care Circle participants read work they had written together, by choice, for the first time in public.

## Why we do these

Being seen changes people. In our post-program surveys, participants who performed or exhibited in public reported the biggest jump in confidence of anything we ran this year. Audiences, too: most of the 300 had never been to an arts event.

## Thank you

To Community Arts Centre for the hall, Local Radio Collective for the sound, and everyone who paid at the door. Showcase Nights return in 2026 as the final milestone of every program.
