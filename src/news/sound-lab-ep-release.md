When the Sound Lab opened in 2025 it had one laptop, a donated audio interface, and a microphone on loan from a member's uncle. This month, nine of its producers released a six-track EP as a collective.

## How it happened

The idea came from the circle, not from us. Members had been trading beats for months and wanted a reason to finish something. A mentor set a deadline. The deadline did the rest.

Each track was produced by a different member, with vocals from across the group. Mixing happened on the shared laptop in two-hour slots, booked on a whiteboard.

## What it means

Two of the producers have since been booked for paid sessions. Local Radio Collective has the EP in rotation and has offered the lab a residency in June. And the whiteboard is full again.
