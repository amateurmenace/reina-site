[Creative Care Circles](/programs/creative-care-circles/) began as a single weekly group in 2025. From this month they run in three community centres across Harare.

## What changed

Demand. Facilitators were turning people away, and the thing that makes a circle work, the same small group every week, means you cannot just make the room bigger.

So we trained four new facilitators in psychosocial first aid and creative facilitation, built a referral network with two partner organisations for anyone who needs more support than a circle can offer, and opened two new groups.

## If you need somewhere to be

Circles are free and open to young people aged 15 to 30. You do not have to be an artist. You do not have to talk. Come on the day, or message us first if that is easier, from the [contact page](/contact/).
