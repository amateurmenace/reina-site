Our 2025 annual report is published today on the [transparency page](/transparency/).

## What is in it

- Who we reached, by program, age, gender, and location.
- Outcomes from our post-program surveys, including the ones that disappointed us.
- Full income and expenditure, with the allocation we promise on the [donate page](/donate/) reconciled against what actually happened.
- A governance section: board changes, policies adopted, safeguarding cases handled.

## The short version

More young people, better outcomes on confidence and wellbeing, and a livelihoods number we want to move faster. Read it, and tell us what you think.
