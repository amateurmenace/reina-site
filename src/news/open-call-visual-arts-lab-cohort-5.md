Cohort 5 of the [Visual Arts Lab](/programs/visual-arts-lab/) opens in February 2026, and applications are open now.

## What you get

- Twelve weeks of studio time with practising mentors, twice a week.
- All materials provided. Travel support where it is a barrier.
- A paid credit on a public community mural.
- A finished portfolio and a showcase night to launch it.

## Who should apply

You are 16 to 28, live in or near Harare, and want to make visual work. You do not need experience, equipment, or a certificate. We prioritise applicants from low-income households, young women, and young people with disabilities.

## How to apply

Use the [get involved form](/get-involved/?program=visual-arts-lab) and choose "Join a program". Tell us, in a few lines, what you want to make and why. Applications close on 31 October. We will reply to everyone by 15 November.
