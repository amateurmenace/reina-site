Six months ago, thirty young people joined the [Youth Voices Forum](/programs/youth-voices-forum/) with a question: what would change in my community if someone actually listened?

This month they found out.

## What they presented

- **Harare:** a mural-and-signage plan to make a notorious footpath safer, co-designed with residents.
- **Bulawayo:** a weekend creative market on an unused council plot, with a youth-run management committee.
- **Mutare:** a schools exhibition programme bringing student art into public buildings.

## What happened next

Harare's ward council adopted the footpath plan immediately and asked the fellows to lead implementation. Mutare's proposal was referred to the education committee with a recommendation to approve. Bulawayo's is under review, with a pilot date being negotiated.

"We did not expect a yes in the room," one fellow told us. "We expected a form."
