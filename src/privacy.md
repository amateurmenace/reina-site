## Who we are

Resources of Empowerment through Innovation and New Artistry (REINA) is a youth-led organization based in Zimbabwe. This policy covers this website and the forms on it. Contact us about privacy at [{{ site.contact.email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact.email }}).

## What we collect

- **Forms.** When you contact us, sign up for the newsletter, or apply to join, volunteer, mentor, or partner, we keep what you type: name, email, phone (if given), location, and your message. We also record which link brought you to the site (for example "instagram"), so we know which channels work.
- **Analytics.** We may use a privacy-friendly analytics tool to count visits and see which pages and referral sources are popular. Where we use a cookieless tool, nothing is stored on your device. If we enable Google Analytics, IP addresses are anonymized.
- **Visitor and engagement counters.** The counters on this site use aggregate numbers and a small record in your own browser so you are not counted twice in a day. That record never leaves your device.
- **Donations.** Payments are handled entirely by our payment providers. We receive your name, email, amount, and a transaction reference so we can send a receipt. We never see or store card numbers.

## How we use it

To reply to you, run programs you applied for, send the newsletter you asked for, issue donation receipts, and report aggregate numbers (never individuals) to funders and the public.

## Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only with service providers who process it for us (email, forms, payments, analytics) under their own privacy terms, or when the law requires.

## Your rights

Ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete your data, or to stop the newsletter. Email [{{ site.contact.email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact.email }}) and we will act within 30 days.

## Children and young people

Many of our participants are under 18. We collect only what a program needs, with consent from the young person and, where required, a parent or guardian. Photos and stories of young people are published only with written consent.

## Changes

We will post any changes here with a new date.
