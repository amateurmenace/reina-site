## Donations

Donations to REINA are voluntary gifts and are generally non-refundable. We put funds to work quickly, in line with the allocation shown on the [donate page](/donate/).

## When we will refund

We will refund in full, no questions asked, if you ask within **14 days** of giving and any of these apply:

- the amount was entered by mistake (for example, an extra zero);
- a recurring gift was charged after you cancelled it;
- the gift was made without your authorization.

Email [{{ site.contact.email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact.email }}) with the date, amount, and transaction reference. Refunds are returned to the original payment method, usually within 10 working days, less any fees the provider does not return.

## Cancelling a monthly gift

You can cancel a monthly gift at any time through the link in your receipt email or by emailing us. Cancellation takes effect before the next scheduled charge.

## Tickets, merchandise, and services

If we sell event tickets or merchandise in future, refund terms will be stated at the point of sale and added here.
