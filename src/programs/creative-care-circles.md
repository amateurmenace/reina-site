## Creativity as care

Not every young person who walks into REINA wants a career in the arts. Some need somewhere to be. Creative Care Circles are small, consistent, facilitator-led groups that use making, moving, and telling as a way through hard seasons.

Facilitators are trained in psychosocial first aid and work with a referral network for anyone who needs more than a circle can offer.

## What a session looks like

An hour and a half. A check-in. A creative prompt. Time to make. Time to share, if you want to. A check-out. The same people, the same day, every week.
