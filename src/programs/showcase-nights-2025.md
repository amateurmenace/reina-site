## What we learned

Platforms matter. Participants who performed or exhibited in public reported a bigger jump in confidence than any other activity we ran in 2025. Audiences came back. Local media covered two of the four nights.

## By the numbers

Four nights, 62 young performers and exhibitors, over 800 audience members, and two paid bookings for Sound Lab members that came directly from being seen.

## What is next

Showcase Nights return in 2026 as part of every program's final milestone rather than a stand-alone series.
