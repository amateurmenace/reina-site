## A studio and a circle

The Sound Lab is two things at once: a working studio where members learn production, songwriting, and recording, and a weekly mentorship circle where the same people talk honestly about what is going on in their lives.

That pairing is deliberate. Music is how many of our members process a hard year, and the circle makes sure nobody does it alone.

## Equipment and access

Members book studio time through the coordinator. Gear is shared and maintained by the members themselves, who learn to run sessions for each other.

## Outputs

A collective EP a year, regular showcase nights, and a growing library of member releases we promote through our channels.
