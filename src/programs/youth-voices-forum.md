## From voice to plan

Fellows spend six months learning to listen, research, and lead. Each cohort picks an issue in their own community, runs listening sessions, and designs a creative response: a campaign, an exhibition, a performance, a piece of public art.

Then they present it. Not to us, but to the people who can act on it: ward councillors, school heads, local business owners.

## The leadership curriculum

Ethics, facilitation, budgeting, public speaking, and the practical politics of getting something done in a community. Taught by people who have done it.

## Why it matters

Our third objective is a new generation of ethical, innovative, socially conscious leaders. This is where it starts.
