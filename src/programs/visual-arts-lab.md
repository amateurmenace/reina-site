## What happens in the lab

Twice a week for twelve weeks, a cohort of young artists works in a shared studio with practising mentors. The first half builds fundamentals: drawing, colour, composition, and the habit of finishing. The second half is a live brief: a community wall, negotiated with residents, designed by the cohort, and painted together.

Every participant leaves with a portfolio, a paid credit on a public artwork, and a peer network that outlasts the program.

## Who it is for

Young people aged 16 to 28 who want to make visual work and have not had access to materials, mentors, or a space to do it. We prioritise applicants from low-income households, young women, and young people with disabilities. No experience is required.

## What it leads to

Graduates have gone on to run their own community wall projects, sell work at local markets, and return as assistant facilitators. See the [impact page](/impact/) for outcomes.
