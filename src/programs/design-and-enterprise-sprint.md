## From craft to income

Four weeks, one goal: make a first sale. Participants arrive with a skill (illustration, textiles, photography, music production, crafts) and leave with a product, a price, a brand, and a customer.

## Sessions

Week one: who is this for. Week two: make it and cost it. Week three: brand it and show it. Week four: sell it, at a market day we run with partners.
